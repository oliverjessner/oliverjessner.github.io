Four workflows. Zero platform theater.

One reader. Four ways to cut the noise.

NO-BULLSHIT-RSS is not trying to become a news platform. It gives you a feed, grouped digests, transparent local topics and direct CLI access to the same database.

01 Feed 02 Digest 03 Topics 04 CLI
01 / Feed

A feed that stays a feed.

See what is new. Decide what matters. Move on.

  • Chronological means chronological. No ranking algorithm decides what should be important.
  • Search articles and filter by topic, source or reading list.
  • Switch between card and compact layouts.
  • Save, dismiss or open the original article directly.
02 / Digest

Many links. One story.

Related coverage from multiple sources becomes one readable story cluster.

See who covered the same event, when the first and latest reports appeared, and how many sources belong to the story. Choose a daily, weekly or monthly view.

Multiple sourcesDaily / Weekly / MonthlySave storyMark as digested

This is organization, not an AI-generated summary feed. The Digest clusters articles already stored in your reader.

03 / Local topics

Your topics. Your rules.

Topic detection without sending your feed away.

Define your own taxonomy with editable strong, medium and weak keywords. Articles are classified locally, then become filterable in your feed.

AIlocal rule
StrongOpenAIChatGPTLLM
MediumCopilotGeminiPrompting
WeakModelInference

No external classification API. Edit rules and reprocess existing articles whenever you want.

Source control

Your sources. Not somebody else’s platform.

Add, test, edit and remove RSS feeds. Organize the input, see stored article counts and fetch on your schedule.

04 / CLI

Your RSS database has a CLI.

Use the same articles and digests from the desktop reader directly from the shell. SQLite opens read-only; the CLI never configures feeds, fetches content, starts the app or writes to the database.

NO-BULLSHIT-RSS — read only
$ no-bullshit-rss articles last 10 --url $ no-bullshit-rss articles last 10 --title $ no-bullshit-rss articles last 10 --url --title $ no-bullshit-rss articles digest 10 --daily $ no-bullshit-rss articles digest 10 --weekly $ no-bullshit-rss articles digest 10 --monthly $ no-bullshit-rss articles last 10 --url --title | grep Nvidia
