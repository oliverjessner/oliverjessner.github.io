Who it is for

Keep sources you can search later.

Journalists and researchers

Save a video source. Find a spoken phrase or visible text, then review the matching moment.

Creators and media collectors

Save your own clips as video, audio or text. Group related items with tags and browse their creators.

Features

Download, search and understand saved videos.

Video and audio

  • Save supported yt-dlp links from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and other platforms.
  • Choose Best or Max 1080p video, MP3 or Opus audio.
  • Use a supported link timestamp to start from that point.

Captions, subtitles and transcripts

  • Post captions: descriptions, optionally exported as .caption.txt.
  • Creator subtitles: available original tracks, with language selection and copying in Video Details.
  • Transcripts: local faster-whisper speech recognition on macOS 14+, with optional timestamps and video retention.

AI Summary & Ask this Video

Open AI Video Summary in a saved video’s details to explore its transcript.

  • AI Summary: choose Analyze Video for a Quick Summary and Key Takeaways. Copy the result or regenerate it when needed.
  • Ask this Video: ask questions about what was said and follow up on the details. Start chatting without generating a summary first.
  • Reopen saved summaries and conversations alongside the video in your local History.

Requires a saved transcript, an installed and authenticated Codex CLI, and internet access. AI requests send transcript content to the service used by Codex when you analyze or send a question.

Playlists and a persistent queue

  • Add links, a YouTube playlist or a TXT list of YouTube, TikTok and Instagram links.
  • Choose Auto-start or start the queue yourself. Waiting jobs and settings survive restarts.
  • Use Queue again for interrupted jobs. Completed downloads are not automatically repeated.
  • Magic Import reads a supported clipboard link when you click the logo with an empty URL field.

Search your transcripts

In History, open Filters → Search in → Transcript. Enter the words you remember, then open a result to read or copy its text.

Combine searches with Source and Tag filters. History also shows download counts, recorded file sizes and runtime.

Tags for saved media

Create colored tags and assign several to an item. Browse a tag’s saved content and creator accounts, or filter History by tag.

Keywords and text occurrences

Compare recurring words across transcripts, subtitles, captions and OCR. Filter by text source and follow a word to its saved occurrences.

Creator accounts

  • Choose View account in Video Details to see a creator’s saved downloads.
  • Open the profile or browse public videos to prepare another download.
  • Load available public Social Insights manually, then read the saved snapshot offline.

Online Social Insights require macOS 13.5+. Profiles and metrics depend on platform support and public availability.

OCR analysis for saved videos

Open a saved video’s OCR Text tab. Apple Vision recognizes visible text locally on your Mac.

  • Search recognized text and inspect its timestamped frame.
  • Copy all text, remove repeated results or delete individual frame text.
  • Keep the video when downloading a transcript; OCR cannot run on TXT or audio alone.

Inside the app

Your media and saved text in one place.

Inputs, outputs and limits

Know what each download keeps.

Bring links. Keep files and text.

  • Input: supported HTTP(S) video links, YouTube playlists and supported TXT lists.
  • Output: video, MP3, Opus or TXT transcripts, with optional caption files, subtitles and thumbnails.
  • Local library: source details, saved text, tags and requested insight snapshots.

Availability varies by source.

  • Downloads depend on yt-dlp, the platform and your access to the media.
  • TXT, Magic Import and Browser Import each have narrower rules than manual links.
  • Speech and OCR results can contain mistakes. Check the source before quoting.

Check supported links and caption behavior.

CLI and automation

Add downloads from your terminal.

Configure the output folder and yt-dlp in the app first. Replace the example URL with your own link.

Queue a link and check the result.

/Applications/PineFetch.app/Contents/MacOS/PineFetch queue add --link 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID' --preset 'mp3'
/Applications/PineFetch.app/Contents/MacOS/PineFetch queue list
/Applications/PineFetch.app/Contents/MacOS/PineFetch history list
/Applications/PineFetch.app/Contents/MacOS/PineFetch stats

All CLI commands, presets and setup.

The desktop app does the work.

  • The CLI connects to PineFetch and starts the app when needed.
  • Your CLI preset, saved output settings and the current queue mode apply.
  • History lists the latest 25 successful downloads and is read-only.
  • queue remove removes a waiting item; it does not cancel an active download.

Integrations

Send links from your browser or tools.

PineFetch Link Dump

  1. Install the Chrome extension.
  2. Enable Settings → Browser Import in PineFetch.
  3. Generate a connection secret and pair the extension.

Send a supported link or collection to the queue, or export a TXT list. Browser Import accepts YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X and Reddit links.

The local Link Dump API also accepts links from scripts. The CLI uses a separate local connection and works with Link Dump disabled.

Privacy and local processing

Your library stays on your Mac.

Stored and processed locally

  • Downloaded files, History, saved text, tags, AI summaries, conversations and insight snapshots are stored on your computer.
  • faster-whisper transcribes locally. Apple Vision performs local video OCR.
  • Downloads and initial model setup need internet. Public profiles and Social Insights make network requests when you ask for them.

Optional YouTube browser access

  • No PineFetch account is required. A creator view is a saved profile, not a sign-in.
  • Use Settings → Browser → Connect Browser to opt in to fresh YouTube cookies. Disconnect turns this off.
  • PineFetch saves your browser choice, not cookie values. The check keeps cookie values out of its UI and log.
  • macOS may request Full Disk Access or Keychain permission.

Optional AI: choosing Analyze Video or sending a question makes an online request through Codex with transcript content. Chat also sends your question and recent conversation context. Opening the AI tab reads saved results without starting generation.

Public access checked confirms public metadata access. YouTube sign-in and private, age-restricted or members-only access remain unverified. Cookies do not guarantee avoiding rate limits.

When PineFetch fits

A local media library, beyond a single download.

Choose it for a repeatable workflow.

  • Use a visual queue instead of preparing a command for every link.
  • Keep searchable text and creator context beside saved media.
  • Use the browser extension or CLI with the same desktop queue.

Check the boundaries first.

  • The published installer is for Apple Silicon Macs.
  • Online access and metrics depend on the source platform.
  • Recognition results need review; an insight snapshot is not continuous monitoring.

Get PineFetch

Install PineFetch

Version {{ app.software_version }} for Apple Silicon Macs. Choose Homebrew or the DMG.

  • App and downloads: macOS 11 or later.
  • Local transcription: macOS 14 or later.
  • Online Social Insights: macOS 13.5 or later.

With Homebrew

Install the app, then install yt-dlp separately. The PineFetch cask also makes its CLI command available.

brew install --cask oliverjessner/tap/pinefetch brew install yt-dlp

Need Homebrew? See its installation guide.

Download the app

Open the DMG and drag PineFetch to Applications. Install yt-dlp separately. If it is not detected, set its path in Settings → Diagnostics.

Release notes and download assets

ffmpeg/ffprobe, Deno and the Python/Whisper runtime are bundled. You do not need a separate Python installation. A transcription model may download before first use.

Your first saved video

  1. Choose an output folder in the Download view.
  2. Paste a supported link, choose Best and start the queue.
  3. Open the completed item in History to inspect its files, source and available text.
