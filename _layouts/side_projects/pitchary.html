Two folders, separate records

  • Import PDFs at startup and when files change.
  • Check the status or start a manual scan.

Integrations

Access CRM data through MCP

Connect your MCP client to the local Pitchary database.

Search and create

  • Search for customers, contacts and services.
  • Create records and assign existing tags.
  • Link contacts to an existing customer.

Stay in control of changes

  • Editing, archiving and deletion stay in the app.
  • MCP does not change proposals or financial data.
  • Launch the app first to create the database.

What stays local, what goes to OpenAI?

Local processing

  • Store customers, proposals and invoice data on your Mac.
  • Extract PDF text and read scanned documents locally with OCR.
  • Review and complete invoice data yourself without AI.

Optional AI recognition

  • Enable it with your own OpenAI API key.
  • Extracted invoice text is sent to OpenAI.
  • Internet access and your own API access are required; API costs are additional.

Does Pitchary fit your workflow?

For your own customer work

  • You work independently on a Mac.
  • You want to track contacts, proposals and payments together.
  • You want to manage your data locally.

Know the limits before you start

  • A local, single-user app; no shared cloud CRM for teams.
  • Imports existing invoice PDFs; no invoice editor.
  • Payment tracking and analytics; not a replacement for a full accounting system.
