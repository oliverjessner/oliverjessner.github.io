    - question: 'Does topic detection send articles to an AI API?'
      answer: 'No. Topic detection is local and rule-based, using strong, medium and weak keywords that you can edit.'
    - question: 'What does the Digest do?'
      answer: 'It groups related coverage already in your reader into story clusters for daily, weekly or monthly review. It does not generate AI summaries.'
    - question: 'How does Watchdog work?'
      answer: 'Watchdog checks titles, teasers and stored content against your keywords. Matches appear in a local inbox. It runs with feed refreshes while the app is running, even with its window closed. Quitting stops monitoring. Notifications are optional and need macOS permission.'
    - question: 'Can I use it from the terminal?'
      answer: 'Yes. CLI queries read your existing library without fetching feeds or starting the app. Explicit Watchdog management commands can change rules, match states and notification settings through the running app. Homebrew installs the bundled command; DMG users link its launcher once.'
      button_label: 'CLI setup and commands'

    - question: 'What can I import and export?'
      answer: 'Import article URLs by pasting links or opening a plain text link list. Export Feed, Digest or Watchdog results as Markdown, JSON, CSV or a link list. This is article-link import, not an OPML feed-list import.'
    - question: 'Can I connect an MCP client?'
